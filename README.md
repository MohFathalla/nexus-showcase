# NEXUS — Process Safety Knowledge Platform


### Technology Snapshot

**React • TypeScript • NestJS • PostgreSQL • Prisma • REST API • RBAC • Enterprise AI**

**Focus:** Process Safety • Knowledge Management • Enterprise AI • Digital Transformation

> **From fragmented safety information to connected operational knowledge.**

NEXUS is an enterprise Process Safety Knowledge Management platform designed to connect critical safety information, operational workflows and organizational knowledge in one governed digital environment.

## The Challenge
Process-safety knowledge is often distributed across documents, departments and disconnected systems. This makes relationships between equipment, changes, hazards, incidents, procedures and safeguards difficult to see and maintain.

## The Solution
NEXUS creates a connected knowledge layer around process safety. Instead of treating safety records as isolated documents, the platform links information across the operational lifecycle.

## Core Capabilities
- Process Safety Information (PSI) and document lifecycle management
- Management of Change (MOC) workflows and approvals
- Risk, barriers and operational-integrity context
- Incident and KPI tracking
- Procedures and organizational knowledge
- Equipment and organizational hierarchy
- Role-based access control and auditability
- AI-assisted interaction with enterprise knowledge
- Designed for controlled/on-premise environments

## Why It Matters
**Connected Knowledge → Better Context → Faster Decisions → Stronger Safety Governance**

NEXUS is designed to help organizations move from fragmented safety records toward a connected, searchable and governed knowledge ecosystem.
